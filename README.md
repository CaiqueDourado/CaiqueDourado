## Featured Flagship Platform

### [TudoSobreProdutos.com.br](https://www.tudosobreprodutos.com.br)
*Autonomous Product Intelligence & Synthetic Review Platform (2020 / re-architected 2026)*

Brazil's unbiased product testing and intelligence benchmark (equivalent to RTINGS.com & Versus.com for Brazilian commerce). Brands do not pay for ranking positions.

- **17-stage autonomous ETL pipeline:** from raw distributed scraping to structured technical portals and automated video delivery.
- **Scale:** 2.8M+ real user reviews processed across 3,600+ stores, cross-referenced with technical specifications and local certifications (INMETRO, ANVISA).
- **Evaluation methodology:** 4-step process combining spec screening, big data review transcripts, AI failure analysis, and human editorial curation.
- **Automated Video Pipeline:** Neural TTS narration + `Remotion` composition generating video summaries published to YouTube Shorts & TikTok.
- **Agent-Ready Architecture:** Implements `llms.txt`, MCP Server Card, and content negotiation (`Accept: text/markdown`) with a custom 12-step parser stripping 95%+ of UI noise for AI agent digestion.
